Sizing Guide & Direct Support

How to Measure Your Boat, RV or Trailer

1. Boat on Trailer (Add 4–6 Feet to Hull Length): A 26' center console boat typically measures 31' to 33' tip-to-tip once you include the trailer tongue and outboard motor tilted down. Choose our 12'×35' or 12'×40' pad.
2. Fifth Wheels, Travel Trailers & Class A RVs: Include the hitch/gooseneck and rear bumper/ladder. Rigs over 35' or towed behind crew-cab dually trucks fit best in our 12'×40' or 14'×50' Pull-Through spaces.
3. Indoor Roll-Up Door Clearance: All 20 doors on our 30'×100' building measure 9'0" wide × 10'0" high with a 12'0" eave height.
